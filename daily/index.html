How the puzzle works

  1. Your move. Place an anchor from your hand on any empty square, step one of your anchors to a square next to it, or lift one back into your hand.
  2. Anchors pull. Every anchor pulls the pearls in its row and column: by 3 from the next square, 2 from two squares away, 1 from three. A pearl in between blocks the pull, and both sides’ anchors pull alike.
  3. Pearls drift. After each move every pearl slides one square the way it is pulled hardest. Opposite pulls subtract, and when up-and-down equals left-and-right the pearl stays where it is.
  4. Shores collect. A pearl that reaches a shore row is collected: Light’s shore is the top row, Dark’s the bottom. A pearl that slides onto an anchor sends that anchor back to its owner’s hand.

The puzzle gives you one to four moves to force a pearl onto your shore, whatever the other side answers. Each puzzle has exactly one first move that works.

The whole game runs on the same rules. Why the pearls drift sets them out, with the reasons for each.

Questions

Is the daily puzzle free?

Yes. It plays in your browser with no account and no ads. The game itself is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad; one optional purchase, Swan Road Complete, adds the 9×9 board, the full puzzle archive and more.

When does the puzzle change?

At midnight where you are. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day, here and in the app.

Can I play earlier puzzles?

In the app. Its archive holds eighty puzzles, from one move deep to four; the first three of each length are free and the rest come with Swan Road Complete. This page plays today’s puzzle only.

What is Swan Road?

Swan Road is a two-player abstract strategy board game. Five pearls lie between two shores; anchors pull, pearls drift and the tide rises, and the first to pull three pearls home wins. Play the computer at four levels, a friend on one device or online, and a daily game with a leaderboard.

Does this page keep anything about me?

Your browser remembers which days you solved the puzzle, so the page can say so when you come back. Nothing is sent anywhere. On an iPhone or iPad the page plays three daily puzzles and then hands you to the app, where the daily puzzle is free.
