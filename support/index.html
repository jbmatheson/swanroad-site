Swan’s Road support

For iPhone and iPad

Contact

Write to jim@mouseandchisel.com. For a bug, the device model, iOS version and the app version from the About card in Settings make it much faster to fix.

Learning the game

Home → How to play opens a ten-lesson tutorial on a live board, and Settings → Rules has the full rules on one page. The first game against the computer is guided.

Purchases

  • Swan’s Road Complete and the two looks are one-time purchases tied to your Apple ID. They carry over to a new device.
  • If a purchase does not show up, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Online play

  • Online matches, leaderboards and achievements need a Game Center sign-in: iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • A turn notification opens its match. If a match will not load, it offers Resign and remove.
  • Only players on the same rules version are matched, so keep the app updated to find opponents.

Play nearby

Both devices need Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth on and the app open on the Play nearby screen. One invites, the other accepts.

Your data

Games, statistics and puzzle progress live only on your device; deleting the app deletes them. Settings has a button to reset puzzle progress. See the privacy policy.
