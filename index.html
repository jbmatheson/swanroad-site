Swan’s Road

Anchors pull. Pearls drift. The tide rises.

A two-player strategy board game for iPhone and iPad. Learn it in five minutes; play it for years.

Get it on the App Store How it plays

Three ideas, one board

Five pearls lie in an oyster bed between two shores. Pull three onto yours before your opponent does.

One

Anchors pull

Each turn you place, step or lift one anchor. It pulls every pearl in its row and column: strongly up close, weakly from three squares away.

Two

Pearls drift

After each turn every pearl moves one square toward the stronger pull. Equal pulls cancel, so a well-placed anchor can hold a pearl still.

Three

The tide rises

From turn 41 both shores grow deeper, and the tide comes early if the board goes still. Sixty turns at most; a tie goes to the second player.

Everything in the box

No ads, no subscription, no account. Your games stay on your device.

Four AI levels

From a gentle first opponent to an expert that thinks for four seconds a move, and an AI rating that climbs with you.

Play together

Two players on one device, two devices in the same room over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth, or anyone online through Game Center.

The daily game

One setup a day, the same for everyone, one try. Your turns to win go on a Game Center leaderboard that resets at midnight.

A 30-level campaign

Three chapters from the shallows to the long board. New layouts, both seats, and tides that come early or late.

Eighty puzzles

Collect in three, hold the line, win by the tide. Today’s puzzle is free every day, with streaks and achievements.

Learn in five minutes

A ten-lesson tutorial on a live board, a guided first game, pull numbers and a drift preview before every move.

On the phone

Three looks for the board: Night, Nautical chart and Minimal. VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are all supported.
