Swan’s Road

A two-player strategy board game for iPhone and iPad

Five pearls lie in an oyster bed between two shores. Each turn you place, step or lift one anchor. Every anchor pulls the pearls in its row and column, and after each turn the pearls drift toward the stronger pull. Pull three pearls onto your shore before your opponent does, and watch the tide: from turn 41 both shores grow deeper.
